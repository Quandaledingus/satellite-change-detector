# Data

Large satellite files are **not** stored in this repository (see `.gitignore`).
Put downloaded data in this folder; only `README.md` and `sample/*.png` are tracked.

## `sample/`, synthetic

Generated by `python scripts/make_sample_data.py`. Fake landscape, no real-world
location, no license restrictions. `truth_mask.png` is white where the land cover
was deliberately changed.

## Getting real Sentinel-2 imagery (for Phase 2)

Sentinel-2 is a pair of European Space Agency satellites that photograph the whole
Earth's land every few days in 13 spectral bands, at 10–60 m per pixel.

1. Create a free account at the [Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/).
2. Open the Copernicus Browser and pick an area and two dates (ideally the same
   season in different years, to reduce seasonal false positives).
3. Filter for low cloud cover and choose **Level-2A** products (surface reflectance,
   atmospherically corrected).
4. Download the bands you need: B04 (red) and B08 (near-infrared) at 10 m for NDVI.

**Attribution:** Sentinel data is free and open under the Copernicus data policy. Credit
it as "Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data [year]".

Never commit your Copernicus username, password, or tokens.
